# Modal — guidelines

A modal interrupts the flow to demand a decision. Reserve it for actions that are
**consequential or irreversible** — approving a payout, rejecting a transaction —
not for information that could sit inline.

## Do

- **Do** state the decision in the title, as a question: _"Approve payout of 5,000
  chips?"_
- **Do** name both outcomes on the buttons: `Approve payout` / `Cancel`, never
  `OK` / `Cancel`.
- **Do** put the destructive choice on a `danger` button and keep the safe choice
  as the default focus.
- **Do** explain the consequence in the body: what happens, and whether it can be undone.

## Don't

- **Don't** stack modals. If a modal opens another, rethink the flow.
- **Don't** use one for a message with no decision — that's a toast or an inline note.
- **Don't** hide the close affordance; Esc and the scrim both dismiss.

## Accessibility

Built on **Radix Dialog**, so the following are handled and covered by tests:

- **Focus trap** — Tab cycles within the dialog; focus moves in on open and is
  restored to the trigger on close.
- **Escape** closes; clicking the scrim closes.
- **`role="dialog"`** labelled by the title and described by the body; the rest of
  the page is inert (`aria-hidden`) while it is open.
- Body scroll is locked underneath.
